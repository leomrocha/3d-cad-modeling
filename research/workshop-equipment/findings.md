## Comparison

Gas MIG/MAG uses solid wire, a suitable shielding-gas cylinder and regulator. Self-shielded flux uses the wire's flux protection, with slag removal. Both modes are available on the two STAHLWERK welders below; observe the exact wire manufacturer's polarity. Gas MIG/MAG is proposed for thin automotive steel sheet, self-shielded flux for portable/outdoor work, and MMA for thicker sections according to a defined joint and electrode procedure.

The 160 M is the lower purchase-price option; the 200 ST accommodates 5 kg spools and has a spot function. Optional Lift TIG is DC. Machine input-current ratings are beside their specifications: a 230 V label alone does not determine available output from the workshop circuit.

Factor of duty is the percentage of a standard ten-minute cycle at the stated current and ambient temperature. For example, the 160 M manufacturer claims 15% at 160 A and 40 °C: 1.5 minutes welding in that cycle. Full-day use includes actual welding/rest intervals, so compare duty at the intended current.

For plasma, the two STAHLWERK material tables specify a maximum stainless thickness of 8 mm. The GYS range brochure reports 15 mm maximum stainless for CUTTER45 CT; retain exact 014787/TPT40 version. MachinePro's EUR1362.24 source is a CATEGORY page. A direct product URL was not captured, so this is a category-page price observation.

Compressor matching result: CUT50P requires at least 100 L/min at 4 bar. DRAKKAR seller states delivered150 L/min without its pressure; those data do not establish delivered100 L/min at4 bar. Mecafer publishes146 L/min at3 bar and88 L/min at7 bar; these points do not establish the4 bar flow. Compressor pairing therefore has no supported pass result from the inspected pressure/flow data.
