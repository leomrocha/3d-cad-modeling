## Operation contracts, proposed boundaries and failure cases

1. **CAD-16 — Overlapping photos and controls to cameras, cloud and mesh.** Registered inputs are `photo-set` AND `measurement-set`, outputs `camera-solution`, `point-cloud` AND `mesh`. `reconstruct_metric(photos, measured_controls, camera_config, pipeline) -> cameras, cloud, mesh, transform, report` composes sparse solve, explicit control alignment, dense reconstruction and meshing. Preserve original photos, matching/feature database, intrinsics, sparse/dense products, fitted transform and control IDs. A scale bar supports a global scale; withheld distributed controls assess local error, and a single distance does not determine full orientation and translation to a target frame. Coverage, reprojection, matching source dimensions, surface holes and regional residuals receive separate outcomes. Blurred/moving/reflective surfaces, disconnected camera components or insufficient controls cause explicit partial/failure results. [COLMAP CLI](https://colmap.github.io/cli.html) and [reconstruction assessment](/home/leo/projects/3dmodeling/research/reconstruction.md) document the constituent routes; the composed helper is proposed.
2. **REC-03 — Sparse structure and camera solution.** `solve_sfm(photo_set, calibration, masks, feature_match_config) -> camera_solution, sparse_cloud, database, report` consumes identified images and their camera/preprocessing configuration. COLMAP's documented stages include `feature_extractor`, matching such as `exhaustive_matcher`, and `mapper`; retain camera model, image IDs, features, matches and sparse outputs. Check registered views, disconnected components, residual distributions, weak baselines, moving surfaces and camera poses; reject an alleged global metric frame without external evidence. Input orientations, intrinsics and lens distortion must match the actual cropped/resized images. [COLMAP CLI](https://colmap.github.io/cli.html) and [camera models](https://colmap.github.io/cameras.html) are earlier web-inspected sources; [OpenMVG](https://github.com/openMVG/openMVG) and [AliceVision](https://alicevision.org/) are registered alternatives. Distinguish image-feature reprojection error from independently measured 3D error.
3. **REC-04 — Video to photo set.** `sample_video(video, sampling_policy, preprocessing) -> photo_set, frame_manifest, report` retains source digest, presentation timestamp, original frame index, extraction settings and each output image's preprocessing. The sampling policy evaluates blur, overlap, camera motion, exposure and repeated frames; a fixed rate alone cannot establish viewpoint coverage. No frames or poorly distributed frames cause a flagged result. [Nerfstudio custom dataset](https://docs.nerf.studio/quickstart/custom_dataset.html) documents image/video processing via `ns-process-data`, COLMAP and FFmpeg; that workflow may perform more than frame extraction, so expose intermediate images and avoid silently claiming the full processing command is a timestamp-preserving extraction API.
4. **REC-05 — RGB-D registration and poses.** `estimate_rgbd_poses(depth_frames, calibration, pose_source, settings) -> camera_solution, point_cloud, report` requires depth scale/range, depth and color intrinsics, relative extrinsics, timestamps, frame identifiers and known pose direction. Sensor registration and synchronization are input requirements; odometry estimates a sequence transform and needs an overlap/initialization check. Distinguish camera-to-world from world-to-camera at API boundaries; retain each estimate, invalid mask and calibration. Reject inconsistent units, timestamp mismatch or absent calibration; check known-depth targets and independent loop closure. The [Open3D RGB-D odometry tutorial](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/tutorial/pipelines/rgbd_odometry.html) is an earlier existing-report registration; its exact extrinsic convention requires pinned-version verification.
5. **REC-06 — RGB-D point cloud and TSDF mesh.** `integrate_rgbd(depth_frames, camera_solution, calibration, voxel_length, sdf_truncation, depth_limits) -> cloud, mesh, integration_manifest, report` proposes Open3D `RGBDImage` with `ScalableTSDFVolume`, then cloud and surface extraction. The [Open3D integration tutorial](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/tutorial/pipelines/rgbd_integration.html) is previously web-inspected and documents TSDF and marching-cubes extraction. Require consistent length units, transform direction, color/depth registration, voxel length and truncation; preserve raw frames and poses. Compare coverage, drift and held-out controls across voxel settings. A small voxel does not supply accuracy: calibration, registration, sensor noise and view coverage constrain it. Wrong depth divisor, invalid depth, inverse pose or empty integration must yield identifiable failures rather than a metric claim.
6. **REC-10 — Learned depth, poses and cloud.** Registered input is `photo-set` OR `video`; outputs are `camera-solution` AND `point-cloud`. `infer_da3(input_variant, selected_model, checkpoint, preprocessing, config) -> camera_solution, cloud, evidence, report` preserves input IDs, timestamps, model and checkpoint revisions/terms, intrinsics/pose convention, confidence and scale status. [Depth Anything 3](https://github.com/ByteDance-Seed/Depth-Anything-3) and [DA3 Streaming](https://github.com/ByteDance-Seed/Depth-Anything-3/blob/main/da3_streaming/README.md) are existing-report registrations for learned depth/pose, streaming and representations. A Gaussian, mesh or GLB is a model/adapter-dependent derivative and does not change the registered required outputs. Metric-model depth is an estimate to compare with independent controls. Check unsupported video/frame adapter, model weights, absent poses, memory errors and low-confidence or conflicting depths; [DA3 Blender add-on](https://github.com/xy-gao/DA3-blender) is separately licensed and its local report records an unresolved add-on licence.
7. **REC-11 — NeRF or Gaussian visual scene.** `train_visual(photos, cameras, selected_method, config, checkpoint_policy) -> visual_scene, training_manifest, report` emits the chosen representation and retains camera conversion, image transforms, data split, code/version, model settings and checkpoint. The existing [Nerfstudio custom dataset](https://docs.nerf.studio/quickstart/custom_dataset.html) documents `ns-process-data images|video` and [Splatfacto](https://docs.nerf.studio/nerfology/methods/splat.html) documents Splatfacto training/export; a supplied camera solution requires a compatible documented importer, not an accidental second camera solve. Evaluate held-out appearance and camera/image correspondences separately from geometry; no geometric derivative follows solely from a scene checkpoint. The generic [geometry export](https://github.com/nerfstudio-project/nerfstudio/blob/main/docs/quickstart/export_geometry.md) and Splatfacto-specific FAQ conflict on mesh/point-cloud export. Check the exact version and model path instead of promising a universal export.
8. **REC-17 — Dense point cloud and mesh from sparse poses.** `dense_mvs(photos, camera_solution, mask_config, dense_config) -> dense_cloud, mesh, workspace, report` validates image IDs, camera model and scales, then retains undistorted images, poses, depth maps and fusion settings. A documented COLMAP path uses `image_undistorter`, `patch_match_stereo` and `stereo_fusion`; an additional supported meshing stage is required for the registered mesh output. A documented OpenMVS alternative consumes compatible `image_undistorter` output with `InterfaceCOLMAP`, then `DensifyPointCloud` and `ReconstructMesh`; OpenMVG is another importer path. [OpenMVS usage](https://github.com/cdcseacave/openMVS/blob/develop/docs/wiki/Usage.md), [OpenMVG/OpenMVS handoff](https://openmvg.readthedocs.io/en/stable/software/MVS/OpenMVS/) and [COLMAP CLI](https://colmap.github.io/cli.html) were previously web-inspected. Missing dense/stereo hardware, import mismatch, absent normals or failed mesh generation produce partial results; sparse output alone does not satisfy both outputs.
9. **REC-18 — Single-image mesh proxy.** `infer_proxy(photo_set, selected_image_id, model, checkpoint, mask_or_preprocessing, config) -> mesh, lineage, report` consumes exactly one selected image from the photo-set, preserving original and mask/crop/background treatment. [TripoSR README](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSR/blob/main/README.md) previously documents `python run.py <image> --output-dir <dir>` and a mesh route. [TRELLIS.2](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2) is an existing-report registration for an alternative mesh/GLB; verify model-specific runtime and asset output. [SAM 3D Objects](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-objects) prior notes describe image-plus-mask inference and Gaussian PLY, so require evidence for its selected version's actual mesh export before assigning it as a direct implementation of this mesh contract. Generated unseen surfaces, topology and global scale are inferred. Test source-view silhouette and independent views/measurements; record backend/weight/license, random seed, failed inference and missing output.
10. **REC-19 — Gaussian training and scene export.** `train_gaussian(photos, cameras, model_config, weights) -> gaussian_scene, checkpoint, report` retains named image/pose mapping, camera convention, normalization transform, training config, code/version and Gaussian export. [Splatfacto](https://docs.nerf.studio/nerfology/methods/splat.html) previously documents Gaussian PLY export and reports mesh/point-cloud export unsupported. A Gaussian PLY and a geometric point cloud can use the same filename extension while carrying different fields and semantics; record the format/schema and reader. Check held-out views, scene framing and registered scale evidence; failed training/export or mismatched poses are failures, not mesh outputs.
11. **REC-20 — NeRF training and scene export.** `train_nerf(photos, cameras, selected_method, config) -> nerf_scene, checkpoint, report` preserves images, normalization, cameras, config, weights/checkpoint and version. [Nerfstudio custom dataset](https://docs.nerf.studio/quickstart/custom_dataset.html) documents `ns-train nerfacto --data <processed-data>` for its route. A checkpoint/viewer scene represents a learned radiance field; supported meshing or point extraction requires a separate version- and model-specific stage, with controls and a separate geometry report. Compare held-out images, coverage, pose consistency and independent geometry where extraction exists. Invalid rays or misregistered frames fail before claiming a scene.
12. **DOM-20 — Photos to cameras and cloud.** Although its title mentions video and mesh, the registered input is `photo-set` and outputs are `camera-solution` AND `point-cloud`. `domain_photo_reconstruct(photo_set, configuration) -> cameras, cloud, report` can compose REC-03 with supported dense sampling; require a clear sparse/dense label in the output. Video enters via REC-04; mesh is a separately documented downstream REC-17/DOM-28 output. Retain raw images, coordinates, controls if supplied, and sources. Verify model/pose alignment and scene component coverage. [Reconstruction assessment](/home/leo/projects/3dmodeling/research/reconstruction.md) describes COLMAP, Meshroom and OpenMVG alternatives; the name does not add unregistered output requirements.
13. **DOM-21 — Depth frames to registered cloud.** Registered inputs are `depth-frames` AND `measurement-set`; registered output is `point-cloud`, even though its title mentions mesh. `domain_depth_cloud(frames, measurements, calibration, pose_source) -> cloud, report` composes REC-05/06 where their extra prerequisites exist, preserving calibration and control evidence and distinguishing sensor metric scale from validated accuracy. Check held-out lengths, pose drift, occlusion and measurement units. A triangle mesh is a separate REC-06 or DOM-29 output. Open3D and [CloudCompare](https://github.com/CloudCompare/CloudCompare) are documented alternatives in the [reconstruction assessment](/home/leo/projects/3dmodeling/research/reconstruction.md).
14. **DOM-22 — Gaussian scene.** Registered inputs are `photo-set` AND `camera-solution`, and registered output is `gaussian-scene`; title text also mentions NeRF, whose separate output contract is DOM-30. `domain_gaussians(photos, cameras, config) -> gaussian_scene, lineage, report` connects REC-19, image/pose validation, training and Gaussian export. Retain scene normalization, display frame, code/weights and reader schema; report held-out rendering independently of physical scale/geometry. [Splatfacto](https://docs.nerf.studio/nerfology/methods/splat.html) supports the registered Gaussian branch in prior source review.
15. **DOM-28 — Dense multiview stereo.** Registered inputs `photo-set` AND `camera-solution` yield `point-cloud` AND `mesh`. `domain_dense_mvs(photos, cameras, settings) -> cloud, mesh, report` uses the REC-17 routes and retains image identity, calibrated pose convention and all scale transforms. Require nonempty dense data and explicit meshing for both outputs; test material coverage and world frame separately. Calibrated intrinsics do not establish the scale of monocular SfM without independent scale/baseline. [COLMAP CLI](https://colmap.github.io/cli.html) and [OpenMVS handoff](https://openmvg.readthedocs.io/en/stable/software/MVS/OpenMVS/) support the recorded alternative stages.
16. **DOM-29 — Point cloud to surface mesh.** `surface_from_points(cloud, method, normal_settings, reconstruction_settings) -> mesh, map, report` retains original point IDs/frame, any filtering and normals; normal estimation/orientation is a prerequisite for methods that use normals. Candidate Open3D Poisson, ball-pivoting or other methods have different assumptions and holes/extrapolation behavior; the exact selected API, parameters and method input requirements need confirmation. Compare directed cloud-to-mesh and mesh-to-cloud errors, holes, connectivity and retained small features; cleanup and decimation change geometry, so retain the pre-cleanup mesh, operation settings and same control measurements. An open or sparse cloud can make surface closure or density unreliable. [Open3D point-cloud operations](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/tutorial/geometry/pointcloud.html) cover point-cloud operations in the earlier source record; meshing and decimation API documentation needs a specific primary-source lookup.
17. **DOM-30 — NeRF scene.** `domain_nerf(photos, camera_solution, training_config) -> nerf_scene, lineage, report` connects REC-20 to an explicit camera adapter and preserves capture, code/weights, data split and checkpoint lineage. NeRF is the registered scene output; optional `ns-export` geometry is an additional supported model-dependent operation with its own receipt. Compare held-out views and pose consistency; validate any geometry with independent measured controls. [Nerfstudio export documentation](https://github.com/nerfstudio-project/nerfstudio/blob/main/docs/quickstart/export_geometry.md) provides general export descriptions, with model-specific support to confirm.

## Proposed future cases; no reconstruction or software tests executed

1. **Photo fixture:** A rigid textured object with calibrated cameras, two distributed fitted controls and additional withheld dimensions should produce the declared cameras/cloud/mesh with a reproducible source-to-target transform. Missing views, repeated texture, moving object, incorrect crop-adjusted intrinsics, degenerate controls and non-matching photo IDs must report the affected component or output. Reprojection error can be low while withheld metric controls fail.
2. **Video and camera pose:** Sample a timestamped clip with known dropped and blurred frames; verify indices, timestamps, image IDs and coverage reporting. A deliberate inverse world/camera transform, axis sign error, pixel-center mismatch or undistorted-versus-distorted mismatch must fail independent reprojection/3D checks.
3. **RGB-D:** Use calibrated synchronized depth/color frames of a known object and known pose sequence, including invalid depth and withheld measurements. Verify depth divisor, direction of odometry and TSDF extrinsics, voxel/truncation settings and mesh/cloud world bounds. Swapped transforms, unsynchronized frames, wrong units, missing calibration and drift cause specific findings; repeated voxel sizes do not substitute for independent controls.
4. **Dense and meshing:** A sparse scene with known image IDs passes undistortion, dense fusion and explicit mesh stages on a supported backend; missing GPU/build support, unsupported importer, disconnected workspace, empty fusion or failed mesher returns partial output. Compare surface residuals, open passages and connected components before and after reconstruction/decimation; preserve the raw mesh.
5. **Learned scenes and proxy:** Provide known held-out views and a pose mapping. The Gaussian export must identify Gaussian fields and checkpoint, and NeRF export must identify its method; attempt unsupported model-specific mesh extraction and expect a reported unsupported/error result. A single image with held-out side/rear views marks hidden geometry inferred; absent mask for a mask-requiring model and unavailable weights/licence yield precise failures.
6. **Representation merge and CAD:** Align named scene, mesh and CAD features using a recorded rigid/similarity mapping and independent controls; compare landmarks and units without claiming shape intent from triangulation. A mirrored frame, double scale, changed source revision, inferred hidden feature or faceted solid presented as a parametric model is surfaced by the proposed checker.

## Source-addendum reread — 12 November 2026

The [primary-source review](/home/leo/projects/3dmodeling/research/action-audit/reviews/point-07-sources.md) and [source records](/home/leo/projects/3dmodeling/research/sources/round-05/point-07-web-sources.json) record 26 inspected original pages and four unsuccessful attempts. The findings below are documentation/source-code claims at the stated version or branch. No project runtime or reconstruction was exercised.

1. **Camera pose and pixel conventions.** [COLMAP output format](https://colmap.github.io/format.html) documents `images.txt` pose as world-to-camera, with Hamilton quaternion `(QW,QX,QY,QZ)` and translation `(TX,TY,TZ)`. Its [camera documentation](https://colmap.github.io/cameras.html) describes +Z look direction and top-left pixel centre `(0.5,0.5)`. The [FAQ](https://colmap.github.io/faq.html) says to add `0.5` to OpenCV/Kalibr integer-centred `cx,cy` when converting to COLMAP's corner-based convention; `fx,fy` and distortion are unchanged. Apply that adjustment only when the actual source and target use those conventions and after recording crop/resize effects; do not adjust already-converted data again.
2. **Sparse/dense and meshing API.** [COLMAP CLI](https://colmap.github.io/cli.html) documents feature extraction, matching and `mapper`, then `image_undistorter`, `patch_match_stereo`, `stereo_fusion` and either `poisson_mesher` or `delaunay_mesher`. The dense stereo route uses an undistorted workspace; fusion creates a colored cloud; mesh creation is a distinct stage. [OpenMVS usage](https://github.com/cdcseacave/openMVS/blob/develop/docs/wiki/Usage.md) documents `InterfaceCOLMAP -i dense -o scene.mvs --image-folder dense/images`, `DensifyPointCloud` and `ReconstructMesh scene_dense.mvs -p scene_dense.ply`, with optional refinement/texturing. The guide also describes CPU semi-global matching with `--fusion-mode -2` and memory-oriented scene splitting, with possible seams. Build, device and selected settings remain explicit conditions.
3. **Open3D depth and trajectories.** Open3D v0.20.0 [RGBDImage.h](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isl-org/Open3D/v0.20.0/cpp/open3d/geometry/RGBDImage.h) declares defaults `depth_scale=1000.0`, `depth_trunc=3.0`, `convert_rgb_to_intensity=true`; [RGBDImageFactory.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isl-org/Open3D/v0.20.0/cpp/open3d/geometry/RGBDImageFactory.cpp) passes scale/truncation to depth conversion and requires equal color/depth dimensions. [Image.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isl-org/Open3D/v0.20.0/cpp/open3d/geometry/Image.cpp) divides raw depth by scale, then zeros values `>= depth_trunc`; zero remains zero. A 1000 divisor converts millimetres to metres only for millimetre-valued source depth, and zero is an invalid sentinel only where the sensor convention supports that interpretation. The [integration tutorial](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/tutorial/pipelines/rgbd_integration.html) uses `depth_trunc=4.0` in its example and integrates using the inverse of its `camera_pose`. [Odometry API](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/python_api/open3d.pipelines.odometry.compute_rgbd_odometry.html) returns success, a pairwise 4×4 motion and a 6×6 information matrix; the project camera/world trajectory direction must be established by the chosen adapter.
4. **Point-cloud surface and cleanup.** The Open3D 0.20 [PointCloud API](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/python_api/open3d.geometry.PointCloud.html) documents `orient_normals_consistent_tangent_plane(k, ...)` using a neighbor graph; consistent orientations do not establish outward orientation. The [TriangleMesh API](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/python_api/open3d.geometry.TriangleMesh.html) documents `create_from_point_cloud_poisson` returning mesh and density values, `create_from_point_cloud_ball_pivoting` using oriented normals and radii in geometry units, and `simplify_quadric_decimation` targeting triangle count with error and boundary parameters. Preserve normals, radii and original mesh, and measure changes against controls.
5. **Nerfstudio basis and actual parser outputs.** [Data conventions](https://docs.nerf.studio/quickstart/data_conventions.html) describe camera +X right, +Y up, +Z backward and world +Z up; per-frame `transforms.json` stores camera axes as matrix columns and camera origin in the translation column. COLMAP/OpenCV camera-space conversion flips Y and Z relative to that basis; image convention, transform direction and resizing remain source-dependent. The inspected mutable-main [Nerfstudio parser](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nerfstudio-project/nerfstudio/main/nerfstudio/data/dataparsers/nerfstudio_dataparser.py) uses configurable orientation/centering and auto-scaling, emits `dataparser_transform` and `dataparser_scale`, incorporates supplied `applied_transform`, `applied_scale` and `orientation_override`, and supplies a documented fallback mapping for a detected COLMAP dataset without `applied_transform`. `scene_scale` determines scene-box bounds; it is distinct from the camera pose scale. Preserve the actual parser configuration, returned transform and scale and metadata; invert the recorded composed transform and account for scale separately to map back to input coordinates. Do not apply a second assumed axis/scale conversion.
6. **Nerfstudio training/export.** [Custom-data documentation](https://docs.nerf.studio/quickstart/custom_dataset.html) describes `ns-process-data images|video` using COLMAP and FFmpeg and separately documents supplied-pose parsers. [Splatfacto](https://docs.nerf.studio/nerfology/methods/splat.html) documents `ns-export gaussian-splat` PLY and states mesh/point-cloud export unsupported; the generic [geometry exporter](https://docs.nerf.studio/quickstart/export_geometry.html) claims TSDF mesh export across models. Preserve that source conflict and establish actual selected model/version support before any such geometry handoff. Gaussian PLY, geometric point-cloud PLY and triangle mesh have distinct schemas and purposes.
