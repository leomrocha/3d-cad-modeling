## Findings

1. **Planar rectification (CAD-02, DOM-02):** [OpenCV geometric transforms](https://docs.opencv.org/4.13.0/da/d54/group__imgproc__transform.html) documents `getPerspectiveTransform` computes a 3×3 perspective matrix from exactly four pairs of corresponding quadrangle points. [OpenCV calib3d](https://docs.opencv.org/4.13.0/d9/d0c/group__calib3d.html) documents `findHomography` as estimating a planar projective transform from paired source/target plane points; its robust methods use four-point subsets and discard collinear subsets, with an inlier mask and reprojection threshold. A length value alone supplies no point correspondences and cannot define a general homography. To obtain metric coordinates, provide identified points in the target plane with sourced coordinates and units; source points must lie on the same modeled plane. Intrinsic camera matrix and distortion coefficients are separate inputs to `undistort`, which compensates radial and tangential lens distortion. OpenCV image warps sample in reverse from destination pixels to source pixels; `warpPerspective` accepts a 3×3 matrix, destination size, interpolation and border options, and supports an inverse-map flag. Preserve the source-to-target matrix, inverse mapping, crop/resize/rotation composition, output grid, interpolation and unmapped regions. Use separate points or independent lengths to check the rectification and metric scale.
2. **Dimension-text candidates (CAD-03, DOM-02):** [Tesseract command documentation](https://tesseract-ocr.github.io/tessdoc/Command-Line-Usage.html) shows TSV hierarchy fields, pixel `left/top/width/height` boxes, `conf` and `text`; hOCR output includes word `bbox` and `x_wconf` metadata. The page describes Tesseract 5 engines and embeds one example from `5.0.1-64-g3c22`; it does not identify the locally selected binary or trained data. Retain exact raw TSV/hOCR and source-image mapping. Confidence and text remain recognition outputs; glyph identity, decimal mark, unit, dimension symbol, arrows and the measured feature need source review.
3. **Raster strokes and vectors (CAD-04):** [OpenCV contour documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/4.13.0/d3/dc0/group__imgproc__shape.html) defines `findContours` as finding contours in an 8-bit single-channel binary image and returns contour coordinates plus hierarchy. [Potrace's 2003 algorithm paper](https://potrace.sourceforge.net/potrace.pdf) describes tracing a binary bitmap into smooth Bezier contours using polygons internally. Potrace states that tracing cannot recover information absent from the bitmap and that multiple outlines may explain the same pixels. These outputs are pixel-derived stroke candidates; they do not identify an engineering feature, true arc, centerline, dimension line or wall-opening type. Preserve thresholding, masks, contour/vector parameters and pixel-space residuals alongside the source image.
4. **Orthographic registration and 3D candidates (CAD-06, CAD-07):** The inspected APIs establish image-plane transforms, lens correction and raster contour extraction. They do not establish view identity, first/third-angle convention, edge correspondence, hidden-face geometry or a unique 3D reconstruction. Register at least two identified views with separate axes, scale evidence and projection convention; carry competing correspondences. Compare projected geometry against independent dimensions and withheld source controls. Solid-kernel validity, projection agreement, dimensional agreement and source-feature identity are separate checks.
5. **Part and wall geometry (CAD-17):** The [FreeCAD Part scripting documentation](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-documentation/blob/main/wiki/Part_scripting.md) identifies its Part data as Open CASCADE B-rep and shows Python primitives/TopoShape construction and assignment to a `Part::Feature`. It establishes a native B-rep geometry route, not raster interpretation, IFC classification or a validity result. Keep reviewed wall/opening graph IDs and source links attached when building solids. Geometry closure/validity and agreement with views and dimensions must be checked independently.
6. **IFC wall and opening (DOM-18):** IfcOpenShell 0.9.0post1 documents `geometry.create_2pt_wall(model, element, context, p1, p2, elevation, height, thickness)`; wall-like geometry follows local +X and thickness follows local +Y, so placement and rotation determine endpoints in the plan. The geometry guide uses metre-valued examples and documents `edit_object_placement(..., is_si=True)` to select SI translation units instead of project units. The example on the [IfcOpenShell 0.9.0post1 `feature.add_feature` reference](https://docs.ifcopenshell.org/autoapi/ifcopenshell/api/feature/add_feature/index.html) first creates the wall and its representation, then creates a separate `IfcOpeningElement`, assigns its geometry, and sets its placement. The subsequent `feature.add_feature(model, feature=opening, element=wall)` call attaches that existing feature to the wall; for the voiding example, the call returns an `IfcRelVoidsElement` relationship. The documentation describes a voiding feature as cutting host geometry; it does not specify when the Boolean geometry is evaluated. Choose semantic openings explicitly from reviewed feature meaning; a Boolean cut alone does not provide an opening relationship or door/window classification. Schema, placement units, wall/opening dimensions, host relation, solid validity and independent consumer readback require separate checks.

## Handoff conditions

Raster rectification can feed OCR and vector extraction only while each result retains reversible source-pixel coordinates through lens correction, crop, resize and perspective warp. OCR tokens and contours remain candidates until a reviewer associates them with dimension marks, line roles and named features. A reviewed plan graph can feed wall/opening geometry only with explicit plan scale, wall thickness convention, floor datum, wall height and opening dimensions/elevations. IFC authoring then needs explicit element classes, spatial containment and opening/host decisions. A valid solid or IFC schema result does not certify the source interpretation.
