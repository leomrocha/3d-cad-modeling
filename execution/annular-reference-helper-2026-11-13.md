1. **Reusable implementation:** `scripts/annular_reference.py` accepts external bore diameter, outside diameter, thickness, XY centre, lower-face position, source bindings and metadata. It constructs two cylinders and subtracts the bore cutter. Component dimensions and source originals remain in the component repository.
2. **Preflight:** required source files and hashes are checked before geometry creation.
