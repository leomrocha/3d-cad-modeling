Third-party Python dependencies retain their own licenses in their installed distributions; they are installed through the pinned requirements file.
4. Canonical reusable source lives under `scripts/` in the 3D CAD Modeling repository. Generated skill copies are recorded by `package-manifest.json` and regenerated by `scripts/build_skill_packages.py`.
