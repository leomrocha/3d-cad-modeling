# Calibrate a 2D drawing trace

Use `scripts/calibrated_trace.py` when reconstructing external geometry from a dimensioned raster drawing and no manufacturer 3D CAD is available. The helper receives source pixel points, a pixel origin, signed per-axis scale, point-pick/origin/scale uncertainties, and optional coordinate units. It returns source pixels, mapped coordinates, and a conservative per-axis worst-case interval. It performs no image tracing and does not choose datums, dimensions, units, or geometry.

Use `calibrated_scale_from_reference(L, d, length_uncertainty_mm=uL, span_uncertainty_px=ud)` to derive millimetres per pixel from a known drawing dimension `L` and its measured raster span `d`. It returns nominal `L/d`, interval `[(L−uL)/(d+ud), (L+uL)/(d−ud)]`, and a symmetric uncertainty equal to the larger distance from nominal to either endpoint. Require finite positive `L` and `d`, finite non-negative uncertainties, and finite, strictly positive lower endpoints `L−uL` and `d−ud`. Reject booleans, overflow, zero or nonfinite calculated scales, and nonfinite results. Record the dimension endpoints and pixel-span endpoints used for `d` in the component recipe.

When `d` is the difference between two picked coordinates, its worst-case uncertainty includes both endpoint uncertainties: `ud=u0+u1`. Keep each image/view's raster size, origin and calibration together. Points read from a resized or cropped display require their recorded transform into the calibrated raster frame. Check a direct source dimension against the normalized profile before creating geometry.

For point `p`, origin `o`, and signed scale `s`, coordinates are `qᵢ=(pᵢ−oᵢ)sᵢ`. The worst-case interval is `uᵢ=u_deltaᵢ|sᵢ|+|pᵢ−oᵢ|u_scaleᵢ+u_deltaᵢu_scaleᵢ`, where `u_deltaᵢ=u_pointᵢ+u_originᵢ`. The final cross-term bounds simultaneous endpoint variation of the pixel difference and scale. The helper also rejects finite inputs that overflow into nonfinite mapped coordinates or bounds. Signed scale controls axis reversal; uncertainty remains non-negative. Reject zero or nonfinite scales, nonfinite coordinates, and negative/nonfinite uncertainties.

Keep the drawing's direct dimension callouts as the controlling values and label traced values as raster measurements with their pixel points, scale, transform, and uncertainty. A raster trace does not establish manufacturing tolerance or hidden wall, fastener, or internal dimensions. Keep those values unknown until another source supplies them.

Synthetic coverage in `tests/test_calibrated_trace.py` checks scale-bound endpoint enumeration, zero uncertainty, invalid denominator endpoints, endpoint overflow and underflow, plus signed-axis mapping, endpoint-enumerated point uncertainty, malformed coordinates and nonfinite outputs. Component-specific feature points and direct dimensions remain in that component's FERME job; pass them as inputs and record this helper's file hash with the generated trace.
