# Exact source profiles and partial construction checks

## Inputs and supported representations

Supply independent source contracts and native readback in the same declared length unit and coordinate frame. Record source hashes, original placements, face/curve identities and numerical predicate tolerances with the caller's job. Keep construction domains and completed physical parts explicitly identified.

1. [Exact profiles](../scripts/exact_profiles.py): `compare_loops` takes complete ordered loops with a caller-classified `role` and `edges`. A Line has `start/end`; an Arc adds `center/sweep` in radians; a NURBS has `degree/control_points/weights/knots/periodic=false/fit_points=[]`. Coordinates are planar XY. Supply positive length and dimensionless parameter tolerances. The adapter supports clamped nonperiodic NURBS, positive weights and expanded knot vectors. Exact whole-loop reversal and cyclic starting-edge changes are allowed. Compare every control, weight, knot, degree and loop role. Preserve original source oriented-edge direction independently of the creation curve's parameter direction. Sampled fallback, interpolation fit points, open loops, altered grouping and ambiguous duplicate matches fail.
2. `signed_extrusion` calculates a signed height from the observed Region normal and source displacement vector in one XYZ frame. Supply a dimensionless angular comparison tolerance. Verify the resulting occupied axial interval against the source before Boolean operations. Two Regions may have opposite normals; each signed height follows its own measured normal. Keep failed operations and checkpoints.
3. [Source solid checks](../scripts/source_solid_checks.py): `halfspace_check` takes XYZ points and `{id, origin, normal, retained_sign}` planes. Sign +1 retains `dot(normal,point-origin)>=0`; sign -1 retains the opposite side. Normalization makes residuals lengths. Check every vertex, source plane and expected retained side. An already retained domain requires a recorded geometric check before skipping a cut.
4. `convex_cell_check` consumes [native analytic features](../scripts/native_acis.py) with `include_edge_curves=True`, caller source vertices, complete supporting halfspaces, expected analytic volume and separate length/volume tolerances. It requires one closed body/lump/shell, reciprocal edges, one straight planar convex loop per face, source vertex bijection, outward normals, source supports, two face incidences per geometric edge and Euler characteristic 2. Boundary integration returns volume in length-unit cubed. Plane direction equality uses a dimensionless 1e-9 predicate. Curved, nonconvex and multi-loop cell faces require a different verified method.
5. ACIS face sense is explicit: SAT `forward` / SAB False retains the surface normal; SAT `reversed` / SAB True negates it. The binary reader exposes `reversed` and planar `outward_normal` on `face_boundary_edges`. Verify sense against raw records when changing decoders.
6. `bilinear_patch_check` takes ordered source and observed 2x2 XYZ corner columns, a source plane and length/Jacobian tolerances. The Jacobian tolerance has length-unit squared. It verifies all four controls, complete planar support and one strict sign at all four Jacobian corners. This covers planar ruled patches. Verify trim edges, endpoint cap loops, cylinders/cones and whole-body coverage separately. Preserve collapsed-edge source bindings; whole-loop collapse and individual edge collapse have separate contracts.

## Material, assembly and failure evidence

1. Derive MATERIAL/VOID membership from the original oriented source boundary. Retain each arrangement cell's witness, source-plane signs and classification proof. A convex hull or fixed chord may enclose a void notch. An additive construction needs verified disjoint interiors, complete source boundary cancellation and agreement with an independent analytic volume integral. A vertex-only or sampled section check does not supply this whole-domain proof.
2. Keep STEP solid definitions, source physical parts, construction domains and expanded occurrences as separate counts. Use [STEP inventory](step-solid-inventory.md), [placements](manufacturer-assembly.md) and [assembly methods](generic-integration.md). Supply units and compose each source placement once. Match native parts bijectively to source parts and compare transformed vertices, analytic surfaces, trim boundaries and metadata independently of counts.
3. Profile similarity permits profile reuse only after complete curve/loop comparison in the transformed frame. Check extrusion depth independently before whole-solid scale reuse.
4. Record native error text, original request ID, raw response/records, source contract hashes and the exact completed/failed stage. Give a cause UNKNOWN when the response merges several internal failure branches. Preserve a verified correction separately. A declared command, source-code branch or passing offline profile check has its own evidence status; native acceptance needs its actual operation response.

## Persistence and displayed views

1. [Save/reopen verification](../scripts/cadtools/verification.py) binds audits to a stable document/revision, then checks the exact OPEN response ID and reopened source path/hash as well as manifests. Entity counts alone cannot bind an audit to the intended document. Use existing same-request recovery; preserve failed receipts and partial saved models.
2. [Capture verification](../scripts/cadtools/captures.py) checks caller-supplied states immediately before/after a viewport capture, its completed response, exact model path, document/geometry/camera revisions, rendered-frame acknowledgement, actual PNG dimensions and file hash. Serialize live requests with the active writer. Identical bytes after a changed camera fail the stale-image diagnostic. Different bytes retain UNKNOWN stale-image diagnosis; `pixel_content_review=REQUIRED` records the separate visual review.
3. For an orthographic isometric viewport, start with a World UCS on the owned preview document. If needed, set it with `UCS World`. Use `UCS 3Point` with origin `0,0,0`, positive-X point `1,1,0` and positive-XY point `-1,1,2`. The resulting plane normal points equally along world +X, −Y and +Z. Use `PLAN Current` and `ZOOM Extents`. Check the live prompts before each input. OpenCADStudio can label the zoom prompt `ZOOM WINDOW` while it still offers the `E` option.
4. Check that the camera is orthographic, with pitch approximately 35.2644° and yaw approximately 45° for this view. Capture with `scope="viewport"` and `wait_for_frame=true`. If the command area overlaps the part, use `ZOOM Out` and capture to a new path. Inspect the image for the complete model and visible features. Verify the source file hash and geometry revision, then close the owned preview document under the normal cleanup procedure. Preserve the original capture and its relationship to any resized gallery image.
5. If opening a large preview reaches the operation query bound, retain the original journal and query the same open request from its original document context. Continue camera setup only after the exact open response and live active path agree. Preserve a capture whose revision and rendered-frame acknowledgement already passed. A later capture with a missing acknowledgement cannot replace it.
6. When a pixel feature looks like a viewport overlay, compare its position across available camera views and project a known model feature using the recorded camera and source placement. Matching movement and position support the feature identity. Use the geometrically matched image for visual review when the whole model is visible. Record the review separately from machine capture verification.
7. For activation and closure, use [guarded cleanup](document-cleanup.md) and [runtime recovery](ocs-runtime.md). Explicit owned IDs, current paths/epochs and a caller-serialized interval precede activation. Recover an uncertain action through its original request ID. Preserve all other document paths/dirty states. Save every meaningful partial construction step and before switching documents; final persistence precedes completion cleanup.
8. For a shaded layer-colour change, read back native RGB layer values and the part assignments. Use `ByLayer` for parts that take their layer colour. A shared definition can use `ByBlock` for leaves that take their individual insert's colour. In OpenCADStudio 2026.42, a fresh shaded viewport capture can still show cached grey faces after successful layer and entity property updates. If the pixels disagree with native readback, run `REGEN` on the same document, confirm the resulting idle revision and take a fresh capture. Check the intended colours on visible part faces. Bind the capture to the post-regeneration state because `REGEN` can advance the geometry revision counter. Preserve any saved-file hash and report the colour inspection separately from geometric checks.
9. Choose the isometric side from the features the image must show. For recesses on the world +Y side, start from World UCS and use `UCS 3Point` with `0,0,0`, `-1,1,0` and `-1,-1,2`. The normal points equally along world +X, +Y and +Z. For an assembly whose rear is world −X and whose inspection side is world −Y, use `0,0,0`, `1,-1,0`, and `1,1,2`. The normal points equally along world −X, −Y and +Z. OpenCADStudio 2026.42 read back yaw −45° and pitch 35.2644° for that rear view. Follow with `PLAN Current`, `ZOOM Extents` and, if needed, `ZOOM Out` to clear the command panel. Verify the eye position relative to the target and inspect the full silhouette and named features in the decoded image. Record the actual camera and image identity.
10. When opposite-side groups share block definitions, assign each installed group’s parent inserts to its own side layer and preserve the nested parts’ role colours. For an isolation check, use an owned preview of the saved model and a fixed camera. Record the side layer on each parent insert, turn off one side, run `REGEN` and capture the rendered result. Repeat for the other side, restore the original layer flags, then regenerate and capture again. Inspect the decoded geometry region in each frame and record the restoration result. Bind this check to the original saved model hash and close the inspection copy after restoring visibility.
11. For a planar XY datum in OpenCADStudio, use World UCS and `PLAN Current`. Read back orthographic projection and a pitch of +90° (`π/2` radians) for the world top view. Use `Wireframe 2D` for line and circle datums and inspect every required mark in the capture.
12. For a layout with clearance solids or datum layers, capture the normal shaded view with its saved visibility. On the same owned preview, show the reference layers and use a 3D wireframe view when opaque envelopes would obscure the installed geometry. Regenerate and fit the complete reference extent before capturing. Record both image hashes, view styles and visibility settings against the same saved model hash. Restore the saved visibility and shading before closing the preview.
13. For an appearance-only delivery copy, compare the stable native entity IDs, types and properties before and after the edits. Exclude only the edited appearance fields from the comparison. Check the reachable block membership and the installed transforms separately. If layer creation requires temporary entities, verify their exact deletion and confirm that no extra live entities remain. Read back the intended layer colours and visibility independently. Before changing an existing layer RGB, enumerate every reachable occurrence whose effective colour depends on it, including hidden and reference leaves, layer-0 inheritance and ByBlock colour inherited through INSERTs. Compare that set with the authorized appearance edits. If an unmapped occurrence would change, keep its existing appearance and use the approved target assignment for the mapped occurrences. After saving, compare the effective RGB, layer and visibility of every affected or protected occurrence with its expected values. Resolve inherited RGB even when the entity properties and visibility flags are unchanged.
14. When verified SAVE writes a separate path and the same owned document stays open, a capture can use that live document without reopening the output solely to check saving. Bind the save receipt and the capture to the same document ID and geometry revision. Verify the live path against the active tab and the output path and hash against the completed save receipt. Check those identities immediately before and after capture. Require a fresh rendered-frame acknowledgement and inspect the pixels. Keep the live source path and saved-output hash as separate fields in the capture record.
15. A completed `SHADEMODE` input can advance the geometry revision before capture. Bind the new view state to the exact completed appearance request and the same owned document and path. Confirm that the saved model hash is unchanged before using the new view state as the capture baseline. Keep the saved geometry revision and the appearance revision separately in the receipt.
16. When a capture lacks the rendered-frame acknowledgement, compare its PNG hash with earlier outputs and inspect the pixels for the expected camera and any previous command prompt. Identical bytes after a recorded camera change and pixels showing a previous prompt establish that the image is stale. Retain the attempt and do not use it as the catalogue image. If the documented refresh sequence fails, preserve the checked model and record the image delivery blocker separately. Close the preview through guarded cleanup after recovery attempts finish.
17. On Linux with OpenCADStudio 2026.42, a stale Wayland viewport can persist in a second Wayland window. If the caller authorizes an alternate preview session and the installed build supports `--new-instance`, test X11 in an owned process. Give it separate XDG config, data, cache and state paths and unset `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` and `WAYLAND_SOCKET` for that process. Use only its new automation descriptor. For a temporary display-only profile on this build, set `settings.default_assoc_prompted=true` and `settings.file_assoc_enabled=false` in its `OpenCADStudio/settings.json`. Use the observed version-matched `settings.donation_prompt_version` value if the installed profile has already acknowledged it. A first-run update notice can be dismissed with the native `cancel` request when the new session contains only its empty start tab. Keep the caller's original process and documents open. Close the owned preview and confirm there are no unsaved documents in the alternate session before ending the owned process. The fallback is qualified by a fresh-frame acknowledgement and inspected part pixels, independently of the previous renderer.
18. On reopening a saved model for an image, inspect the loaded orthographic camera first. If its orientation matches the requested view, retain it and adjust the fit only if needed. To rebuild an isometric from a non-world saved UCS, use `UCS World` before setting `UCS 3Point`. Otherwise the points may be interpreted relative to the saved UCS.
19. Apply the [component completion gate](component-completion.md). Retain failed runs as evidence even when later corrections pass.

### Diagnose a fresh shaded view that disagrees with verified solids

Keep the saved model and its geometry receipt. Check the affected occurrence's full world frame, independently measured feature stations and closed-body topology. On an owned preview of the same revision, hold the camera fixed and compare the affected occurrence alone and in the assembly, in shaded and wireframe modes. Give each capture its own rendered-frame acknowledgement. If useful, compare a standalone source view with the placed occurrence to test whether the discrepancy follows the placement.

When verified placements, measured boundaries and the wireframe agree, but a fresh shaded capture draws displaced or enlarged faces, record a shaded-display discrepancy and the affected representation. The shaded image remains an open delivery issue. Keep the geometry verification result and avoid editing a checked placement in response to the pixels.

If a representation change is authorized, save it as a separate derivative. For a profile approximation, choose and verify a maximum profile deviation independently of the display result. Compare the affected world extents, critical trimmed features and occurrence transforms, and verify the retained geometry before requesting a fresh shaded capture of the derivative. Keep the capture tied to the derivative hash.
