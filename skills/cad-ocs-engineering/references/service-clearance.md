# Check a straight service path

1. Identify the moving occurrence, the saved assembly and every stationary occurrence in scope. Pin their files and measurement records. Compose nested placements into one declared world frame and length unit. Record the service sequence, released parts, start pose, full displacement vector and required clearance. Include connectors, cables, hands and tools when the service requirement includes them and their geometry is available.
2. Supply conservative XYZ boxes enclosing the represented geometry to `translation_box_window(moving_bounds, fixed_bounds, displacement)` in [service_clearance.py](../scripts/service_clearance.py). Boxes are `[[xmin,ymin,zmin],[xmax,ymax,zmax]]`. Use conservative expansions where the required clearance or measurement uncertainty calls for them. The function checks the continuous interval, including contacts at the start and end. `DISJOINT_BOUNDS` establishes separation for the supplied enclosures. `CANDIDATE` gives a closed interval of possible contact. The two exact rational strings give fractions of the full displacement. Parse them with `fractions.Fraction` before evaluating poses.
3. For a move with fixed orientation, project the moving geometry and stationary obstacles onto the same plane perpendicular to travel. Use complete geometry or conservative silhouettes. When using planar mesh triangles, project each finite face and union the results. Preserve edge-on faces as segments or points so they still participate in contact tests. A positive gap greater than the required margin proves separation throughout straight translation for the checked representations. Touching or overlapping projections require a 3D sweep or another geometric proof. Keep mesh, measured surface and envelope scope explicit.
4. Use `shared_definition_coincidence(moving_matrix, fixed_matrix, displacement)` only after verifying both occurrences reference the same nonempty geometry definition. Supply nonsingular affine 4×4 matrices in the same world frame. Identical linear transforms and a matching translation within the finite path give a `COINCIDENCE_WITNESS`. Record the witness travel and both occurrences. For a solid with positive volume, coincidence proves interference at that pose. `NO_COINCIDENCE_WITNESS` leaves the collision question open.
5. Run a supported 3D test for remaining candidates. A positive-volume intersection at one sampled pose proves interference there. Clear samples cover their tested poses. A continuous clear result requires a validated swept-volume or continuous collision method for the whole motion and all in-scope geometry. Keep unknown bodies, invalid input solids and untested clearances visible in the result. A path with rotation, bending or staged motion needs its own supported method.
