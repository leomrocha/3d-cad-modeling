# Rounded hollow-section construction

1. Supply source-pinned section width, height, wall thickness, cut length and inside/outside corner radii in millimetres. Keep standard limits, nominal modeling conventions and measured stock dimensions separately attributed.
2. [rounded_tube.py](../scripts/rounded_tube.py) validates positive straight-wall geometry and constant-thickness concentric corners. Section XY is centered at the origin; cut length is along +Z.
3. Reuse rounded_profile and SelectionCheckedClient for quarter-circle bulges and installed clear-only selection semantics. The bounded-area route creates outer/inner profiles and PRESSPULL in the wall material region; alternative region/subtraction routes retain their explicit operation evidence.
4. Check actual wall planes, corner cylinders, end faces and observed vertex bounds before and after saving. Native SAB decoding uses the bundled [reader](../scripts/native_acis.py). Keep numerical thresholds separate from source manufacturing limits.
5. For transverse bores, [cut_sidewalls](../scripts/plate_reference.py) accepts explicit disjoint cutter intervals, axis, centres and diameter. The caller supplies the geometric proof that an omitted cavity interval contains no material. Preserve exact tangency and source dimensions when testing a bounded recovery. Pass `checkpoint(stage, handles)` to save each new cutter, its completed alignment and the cut wall. The callback raises on failed or uncertain persistence. Verify actual corner axes and radii after drilling. A single cylindrical surface can split into several native faces at a cut.
6. Check each actual cut wall using cylinder_wall_checks and decoded face boundaries, alongside projected axes, body closure, radii and end bounds. Retain failed Boolean responses and prior saved checkpoints. [Incremental saving](../SKILL.md#save-work-in-progress) applies throughout the build.
7. Use `end_bearing_areas` with native-checked tube dimensions and actual mating faces when a tube end seats on a plane. The returned end area covers the whole cut wall. The flat strip area applies when the mating plane spans the section height and its width lies between the native corner tangencies. Verify plane coincidence and actual contact limits before applying either area. Report the resulting nominal area separately from weld throat area and joint capacity.
